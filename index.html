The decision risk

The Quotation Is Only One Part of the Decision.

A sourcing decision can affect quality, inventory availability, cash flow, launch timing, customer experience, management attention, and the amount of capital tied up while mistakes are corrected.

01

The Wrong Supplier

A responsive sales team does not prove quality, capacity, communication, consistency, or problem-resolution capability.

02

The Wrong Country

Freight, tariffs, lead times, manufacturing depth, flexibility, resilience, and regulatory requirements can change the economics completely.

03

The Wrong Assumptions

Certificates, samples, lead times, capacity claims, and other supplier statements should not automatically be treated as verified facts.

No. Final due diligence, samples, certifications, contracts, and commercial negotiations remain the buyer's responsibility.
