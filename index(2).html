FAQ

Clear answers before you order.

Do you receive commissions from suppliers?

No. Global Sourcing Report is an independent research service. Suppliers cannot pay to appear or improve their ranking.

Does a report guarantee a supplier?

No. We organize available evidence and highlight risks and open questions. Customers must still perform final due diligence, request and evaluate samples, verify laboratory reports, and negotiate directly before purchasing.

What products can you research?

We focus on non-regulated categories such as packaging, apparel, textiles, home goods, promotional products, printed products, and private-label goods.

How long does it take?

Most reports are delivered within a few business days after we receive a complete brief and payment.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. Product and business information is treated as confidential and used only to prepare your report.
