Evidence before opinion

What the score does — and does not — tell you.

89/100 is a fit score.

It summarizes observed fit to the requested specification among the candidates reviewed. It is not a probability of success, a verification percentage, or a guarantee.

That distinction matters. GSR keeps verified facts, public-source observations, supplier claims, and unresolved commercial questions separate.

Recommended next actions

What the buyer should do next.

  1. Request a written quote from Eaton for the confirmed quantity.
  2. Confirm the exact SKU and 3-prong / 6-outlet configuration.
  3. Confirm inventory, lead time, unit price, case pack, MOQ, sample availability, and delivered cost.
  4. Request current documentation for applicable certifications and test reports.
  5. Compare all written responses using the same criteria before selecting or paying.
